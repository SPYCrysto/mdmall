# Q1. Experiment 1 – Wireless Frequency Bands (Case Study)

## Aim

To prepare a comparative study of five wireless communication technologies based on their operating frequency bands, communication range, and real-world applications.

## Comparative Study Table

| Sr. No. | Wireless Technology            | Operating Frequency Band                          | Approximate Range                      | Real-World Applications                                    |
| ------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1       | Wi-Fi                          | 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz                             | 20–50 metres indoors                   | Internet access, smart TVs, laptops, and home networks     |
| 2       | Bluetooth                      | 2.4 GHz (2.402–2.480 GHz)                         | Typically 10 metres                    | Wireless headphones, speakers, keyboards, and smartwatches |
| 3       | Zigbee                         | 2.4 GHz globally; other bands in some regions     | 10–100 metres per link                 | Smart homes, IoT sensors, and industrial automation        |
| 4       | Cellular (4G LTE)              | Approximately 700 MHz–2.6 GHz in many deployments | Several kilometres from a base station | Mobile internet, voice calls, and video streaming          |
| 5       | NFC (Near Field Communication) | 13.56 MHz                                         | Approximately 4 cm or less             | Contactless payments, access cards, and device pairing     |

Note: The communication ranges are approximate and depend on environmental conditions, transmit power, antenna design, obstacles, and interference. Cellular frequency bands vary by country and operator.

## Observations

1. Wi-Fi: Provides high-speed wireless connectivity over short distances and is commonly used in homes, colleges, and offices.
2. Bluetooth: Operates in the 2.4 GHz band and is suitable for short-range communication between personal devices.
3. Zigbee: Supports low-power communication and is widely used in IoT applications and smart-home automation.
4. 4G LTE: Provides wide-area coverage and supports mobile internet and voice communication over several kilometres.
5. NFC: Operates at 13.56 MHz and supports very short-range communication, making it suitable for convenient contactless transactions.
6. Higher operating frequencies generally experience greater propagation losses over the same distance. However, communication range also depends on transmit power, antenna characteristics, and the surrounding environment.

## Final Result

The comparative study of five wireless communication technologies—Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, 4G LTE, and NFC—was successfully completed. Their operating frequency bands, approximate communication ranges, and real-world applications were studied.

It was observed that each wireless technology is designed for specific communication requirements. Wi-Fi is suitable for high-speed local networking, Bluetooth for personal devices, Zigbee for low-power IoT applications, 4G LTE for wide-area mobile communication, and NFC for very short-range contactless communication.

Therefore, the selection of a wireless communication technology depends on the required communication range, data rate, power consumption, and intended application.
